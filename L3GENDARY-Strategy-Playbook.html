01 — The Big Picture

What L3GENDARY Actually Is

L3GENDARY isn't just a brand — it's a platform. It combines four things most people keep separate:

🎵
Music
👕
Apparel
📖
Book
🤝
Community

And that's the superpower. Most brands are one-dimensional. You've got four ways in — someone might come for the music and stay for the clothing, or find the book and then discover the community events.

The Core Story (This Is What Sells)

The Hook: "I used to move weight on the streets. Now I move people toward their purpose."

The Truth: Tavius Leverett spent years in the street economy. He made it out — and instead of hiding that past, he built a brand on top of it. L3GENDARY exists because your past is your platform.

This resonates because: Everyone has a past they're not proud of. Everyone wants to know transformation is possible. Your story makes you real in a world of fake.

Who You're Talking To

  • Primary: Young adults (18–35) in urban communities who want to see someone who looks like them winning
  • Secondary: People in transition — reentry, recovery, career change — who need proof that change is possible
  • Tertiary: Supporters & allies — people who want to back a mission-driven brand

03 — Go High Level Setup

What GHL Does for L3GENDARY

Go High Level is the engine behind the website. It captures leads, automates follow-up, and turns visitors into customers without you manually doing anything. Here's the setup:

The Form → Pipeline Flow

  1. Visitor fills out the form on ldl3gendary.com/#join (name, email, phone, interest)
  2. GHL receives the lead and automatically:
    • Tags them by interest (Music / Apparel / Book / Community / All)
    • Adds them to the "L3GENDARY Leads" pipeline
    • Sends a welcome email with the "From the Streets to Legendary" free guide
    • If they gave a phone number: sends an SMS welcome (opt-in required)
  3. Pipeline stages move automatically:
    StageTriggerAction
    New LeadForm submitWelcome email + free guide
    EngagedOpened email or clicked linkSend relevant offer based on interest tag
    CustomerMade a purchaseThank you + cross-sell next product
    AdvocateReferred someone or attended eventExclusive drops, early access, community invite

Automation Sequences to Build

Sequence 1: Welcome (3 emails, 5 days)

  1. Day 0 (immediate): "Welcome to the Evolvement" + free guide download link
  2. Day 2: "The Story Behind L3GENDARY" — your transformation story, link to #story
  3. Day 5: "Where Do You Want to Start?" — links to music, apparel, book based on their interest

Sequence 2: Music Listener → Buyer (4 emails, 10 days)

  1. Top 3 tracks with streaming links
  2. Behind the lyrics: the story behind one song
  3. Bundle offer: all 17 tracks for $4.99
  4. "Artists who wear the brand" — cross-sell to apparel

Sequence 3: Apparel Browser → Buyer (3 emails, 7 days)

  1. Lookbook: Rawlo Apparel in the wild (real photos of real people)
  2. "What your fit says about you" — styling guide
  3. Limited drop alert + direct shop link
Pro tip: The form asks "What interests you most?" — this is your segmentation engine. Someone who picks "Music" gets Sequence 2. Someone who picks "Apparel" gets Sequence 3. Someone who picks "All" gets everything. This is how you sell without being spammy — every message is relevant.

GHL Embed Instructions

In the website file (index.html), find the section marked ⬢ GHL EMBED SLOT in the "Join the Evolvement" section (around line 1130). Replace everything between the ghl-form-slot div with your GHL form embed code. The demo form is there as a placeholder — swap it out and the styling will hold.

Every single post must include a call to action. Not every post is "buy now" — but every post should point somewhere:

• Story post → "Read the full story at ldl3gendary.com/#story"
• Music clip → "All 17 tracks at ldl3gendary.com/#music"
• Fit pic → "Shop the look: ldl3gendary.com/#apparel"

• General → "Link in bio" (and make sure the bio link goes to #join)

05 — The 90-Day Launch Plan

Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

  • Review the website — note any changes you want
  • Set up Go High Level account (or use existing one)
  • Replace the GHL form embed on the site with your real form
  • Build the Welcome automation sequence (3 emails)
  • Update all social bios to point to ldl3gendary.com
  • Create the "From the Streets to Legendary" free PDF guide (or have Claude build it)
  • Post the website launch announcement on all platforms

Phase 2: Content Engine (Weeks 3–4)

  • Film and post 5 short-form videos (TikTok/Reels) — behind the brand, fit check, music snippet, story clip, community feature
  • Go live on Facebook once — just talk about the journey for 15 minutes
  • Post one book excerpt as a carousel on Instagram
  • Build the Music Listener automation sequence in GHL
  • Start collecting email signups — goal: first 50

Phase 3: First Campaign (Weeks 5–6)

  • Run your first limited-time offer: "Rawlo Apparel Launch Week — 20% off with code L3GENDARY"
  • Send the offer to your email list first (early access), then post to social 24 hours later
  • Go live on Facebook + Instagram to show the products and answer questions
  • Track how many sales come from email vs. social — this tells you where to double down

Phase 4: Community Push (Weeks 7–8)

  • Plan and announce a community event (cookout, pop-up shop, backpack drive — something on the ground)
  • Use the event to collect emails and phone numbers in person
  • Film the event — this is content for the next 30 days
  • Invite 2–3 other local business owners or artists to collaborate

Phase 5: Scale (Weeks 9–12)

  • Review what worked: which posts got the most engagement? Which emails got opened? Which products sold?
  • Double down on the winners; cut what didn't work
  • Launch the podcast — first 3 episodes recorded and scheduled
  • Build the Apparel Browser automation sequence
  • Set up abandoned cart recovery in WooCommerce → GHL
  • Goal by day 90: 500+ email subscribers, consistent weekly sales, 1 community event completed

06 — Product Stacking

The Problem

Right now, someone buys one track for $0.99 and that's it. You need them to buy more — without feeling like they're being sold to constantly.

The Solution: Stack Offers Naturally

Tier 1: The Entry Point ($0–$10)

  • Free: Join the Evolvement (email list)
  • $0.99: Individual tracks
  • $4.99: All 17 tracks bundle
  • $8.99: The Book (PDF)

Goal: Get them in the door. The first purchase is psychological — once they've bought once, they're 3–5x more likely to buy again.

Tier 2: The Merch ($29.99–$60)

  • $29.99: Hats
  • $34.99: T-shirts
  • $60: Hoodies & Jackets

Target: Music buyers. After someone buys a track, email them: "You heard the sound. Now wear the movement."

Tier 3: The Bundle ($79–$99 — Coming Soon)

  • "The L3GENDARY Starter Pack": Book + T-shirt + All 17 tracks ($79)
  • "Full Collection": Hoodie + Hat + Book + All tracks ($99)

Bundles increase average order value 40–60%. Set these up in WooCommerce as grouped products.

Tier 4: Premium / Limited ($100+ — Future)

  • Signed book + exclusive hoodie colorway
  • One-on-one mentorship call
  • VIP access to events
  • Custom music feature

The Stack Flow

Music Listener Path: Free track preview → $0.99 single → $4.99 bundle → "You like the music — here's the shirt" → $34.99 tee → $60 hoodie → "You're deep in the movement now — here's the book" → $8.99 book → Event invite

Apparel Buyer Path: Hat ($29.99) → "Here's the music that inspired this brand" → Free track → $4.99 bundle → Tee ($34.99) → Hoodie ($60) → Book ($8.99) → Event invite

Book Reader Path: Book ($8.99) → "The soundtrack to the book" → Free track → $4.99 bundle → "Wear the story" → Apparel → Event invite

The Only Weekly Ritual You Need

Every Sunday, spend 20 minutes checking:

  1. New email subscribers this week
  2. Any sales? What sold?
  3. Which post performed best? (Make more like that)
  4. Which post flopped? (Stop making that)
  5. What's the ONE thing you're pushing next week?

When You're Stuck

Go back to the story. Everything — every post, every product, every email — starts from the same place: "I used to move weight on the streets. Now I move people toward their purpose." If you don't know what to post, tell another part of that story. If you don't know what to sell, ask what helped you in your own transformation and sell that. The story never runs out because you're still living it.
