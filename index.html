CHAPTER 01

The Origin

Every legend starts somewhere. This one started in the streets of Central Florida — and almost ended there too.

Tavius Leverett knows both sides of the line. He spent years in the street economy — moving product, navigating the code, watching friends disappear into the system or the ground. He wrote music about it. He wrote a book about it. And then he made a decision that changed everything: use those same skills to build something that gives back.

L3GENDARY isn't a brand that pretends the past didn't happen. It's a brand built on the truth that your past is your platform — and what you do next is what makes you legendary.

The Book

What happens when a man who's seen the worst of the streets decides to build something better? "A Good Man Who Makes Bad Decisions" is Tavius Leverett's raw, unfiltered account of life on both sides of the line — and the moment he chose to stop surviving and start building.

It's not a redemption story. It's a transformation manual — for anyone who's ever felt trapped by their circumstances and wondered if there's another way.
