THE ORIGIN

The Story

Every legend starts somewhere. This one started in the streets of Central Florida — and almost ended there.

Tavius Leverett spent years in the street economy — moving product, navigating the code, watching friends disappear. He wrote music about it. He wrote a book about it. Then he made the decision that changed everything: use those same skills to build something that gives back.

L3GENDARY isn't a brand that hides the past. It's built on the truth that your past is your platform — and what you do next is what makes you legendary.
